    Greywater Facility · Block A · Day 1

    Night of the Wire

    The Directorate calls Greywater a re-education facility. Everyone inside knows what the East Block is for. Every night another line of people walks slowly through its doors, and the vents on its roof hum until morning.

    By day you work, and every job helps the people who keep you here. Some jobs give you something you can use; the worst job gives you the most. By night the searchlights come on. How you get out is up to you: through the wire, over it, or under it, with your friends or alone, at night or in broad daylight. You can even skip work, if you can stay out of sight. Rumour says Block A will be transferred to the East Block after the fifth night.

    Left thumb moves you; tilt gently to creep. ACT talks, takes and opens; hold it to work. The mode button switches Walk, Sneak and Sprint. GROUP tells your friends to wait or follow.

    On iPhone, switch off silent mode to hear the whispers, the dogs and the siren. Landscape gives a wider view.
