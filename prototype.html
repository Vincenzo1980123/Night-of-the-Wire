What to judge
  • Heads: rounded sculpted ovals; softer noses; eyes set into the face under relaxed lids; calm resting expressions.
  • Shading: Gouraud in palette ramps (finer steps on skin), one light from the upper left; faceting stays on the silhouette.
  • Sets: hand-painted, dithered textures; plinth, corner posts, eaves, sills, step.
  • Interior: bunks, table, stove; roof cutaway and see-through front walls.
